Reel Replies

Turn Instagram comments into conversations. When someone comments a keyword on your Reel, Reel Replies answers publicly and sends them a private message, automatically.

How it works

One keyword, three steps

A viewer comments

You choose the keyword, for example “guide”. Anyone who comments it on a Reel starts the flow.

It replies in public

A friendly reply goes under the comment, picked from several variants so it never looks copy and pasted.

It follows up in private

The viewer gets a DM with the link or message you set, such as your best Reel, a guide or an offer.

Under the hood

Built to run on its own

Reel Replies is a real service, not a script. It has been answering comments on a live account.

Rules engine

Keywords, whole word matching and rotating reply and DM variants, all edited in a simple rules file without touching code.

Safe sending

A rate limited, persistent queue keeps messages going out at a steady pace and retries them instead of dropping them.

Delivery tracking

Every comment is logged with whether the reply and the DM were delivered, and why if they were not.

Admin dashboard

A password protected page to see events, test a rule before it goes live and run health checks.

Verified webhooks

Incoming Instagram events are checked against their signature before anything is sent.

Stays connected

The access token refreshes automatically, so the service keeps working week after week.
