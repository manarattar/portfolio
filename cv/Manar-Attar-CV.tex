% ============ THESIS ============
\section*{Master's Thesis}
\product{Author Profiling of Hate Speech Spreaders}{\color{gray}MA Thesis, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, 2026}
\begin{tights}
  \item \bl{Question}Can the age and gender of hate speech authors be inferred from their writing, and does training on general social media transfer?
  \item \bl{Method}Compared zero shot LLMs (LLaMA-3.1-8B, Qwen3-32B) with BERT, HateBERT and RoBERTa fine tuned on PAN14 (11,906 text chunks), evaluated on 619 English Facebook comments from LiLaH-HAG, against majority class and random baselines.
  \item \bl{Results}Gender: zero shot LLaMA reached a macro F1 of 0.515 against 0.488 for the best encoder and a 0.418 majority baseline. Age: the best encoder, BERT, reached 0.310 against 0.211 for the best LLM. The oldest group (66+, 10.3\% of the test set) was almost never identified by any model. Neither approach is reliable enough for deployment, and the better one depends on the attribute.
  \item \bl{Analysis}Confusion matrix and lexical error analysis suggest the domain as a likely ceiling: hate speech is a narrow, conventionalised register whose shared vocabulary leaves little individual style to separate demographic groups.
  \item \bl{Extension}Extended the study to Slovene with CroSloEngual BERT, where grammatical gender marking gives a morphological cue, gender macro F1 reached 0.609, 9.4 points above the best English result, suggesting that language specific cues can matter more than model size.
\end{tights}
